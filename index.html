Learn Business Intelligence with Phoebe

Two tracks from dashboard-curious to dashboard-fluent

A leader track that teaches you to read, question, commission, and govern dashboards without touching a tool - and a builder track that takes you from your first chart to a star schema, governed measures, a shipped executive dashboard, and a real revenue-dip investigation. Both run on one small warehouse, Daybreak, inside a live mini-BI right in your browser: pick a dimension and a measure, and watch it write the SQL. No install, no login, no license.
